# My GDG WOW Pune Experience

## Introduction

Hello! I'm excited to share my journey and experiences with Google Developer Group Women of Wearables (GDG WOW) Pune. Being part of this incredible community has been a transformative experience that has significantly contributed to my personal and professional growth in the tech industry.

## First Contact

My journey with GDG WOW Pune began when I attended their "Introduction to Wearable Technology" workshop in early 2025. As someone who was curious about the intersection of fashion and technology, this event caught my attention immediately. The welcoming atmosphere and the enthusiasm of the organizers made me feel at home right away.

## Learning and Growth

What I appreciate most about GDG WOW Pune is their commitment to practical learning. Through their hands-on workshops, I've had the opportunity to:

- Learn about sensors and microcontrollers specific to wearable applications
- Work with various development platforms for IoT devices
- Understand the principles of designing user interfaces for wearable technology
- Network with professionals and enthusiasts in the field

The knowledge-sharing environment they've created allows beginners and experts alike to grow and learn from each other.

## Community Impact

Being part of this community has been more than just attending technical events. GDG WOW Pune has created a supportive ecosystem where women in technology can:

1. Find mentors who guide them through career challenges
2. Access resources that might otherwise be difficult to obtain
3. Gain confidence through speaking opportunities and project showcases
4. Build long-lasting professional relationships

## Memorable Events

One of my favorite experiences was participating in the AR/VR Hackathon organized by GDG WOW Pune. Working with a diverse team, we developed an augmented reality application focused on accessibility. The 24-hour event was intense but incredibly rewarding, and the mentorship provided throughout the hackathon was invaluable.

## Personal Growth

Since joining GDG WOW Pune, I've observed significant growth in my:

- Technical skills related to wearable technology
- Confidence in sharing ideas and presenting to groups
- Professional network in the tech industry
- Understanding of the challenges and opportunities in the wearable tech space

## Conclusion

GDG WOW Pune is more than just a tech community—it's a movement that empowers women to break barriers in technology. The supportive environment, quality of events, and passionate community members make it a truly special group to be part of.

I'm grateful for the opportunities and connections I've made through GDG WOW Pune, and I look forward to continuing this journey of learning and growth together.
