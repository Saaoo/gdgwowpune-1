# My Journey with GDG WOW Pune

## Introduction

Hello everyone! I'm excited to share my experience with Google Developer Group Women of Wearables (GDG WOW) Pune. Being part of this vibrant community has been an incredible journey of learning, growth, and connection.

## First Encounter

My journey with GDG WOW began when I attended a tech workshop on wearable technology. As someone interested in emerging technologies, I was immediately drawn to the welcoming and inclusive environment. The session was not just informative but also encouraged hands-on learning and collaboration.

## What I've Learned

Through GDG WOW Pune, I've gained:

1. **Technical Knowledge**: The workshops and study jams have deepened my understanding of various technologies, particularly in the wearable tech space.
   
2. **Networking Opportunities**: I've connected with like-minded professionals, mentors, and industry experts who have provided valuable guidance.
   
3. **Leadership Skills**: Volunteering for event organization has helped me develop leadership and management skills.
   
4. **Confidence**: Presenting my ideas and projects at GDG events has significantly boosted my confidence in public speaking.

## Memorable Events

One of the most memorable experiences was participating in the AR/VR Hackathon. Working with a diverse team to create a solution within a limited timeframe was challenging yet incredibly rewarding. Our project focused on creating an AR application for educational purposes, and the feedback from judges and peers was invaluable.

## Community Impact

What sets GDG WOW Pune apart is its commitment to creating an inclusive tech community. The emphasis on mentorship and support has helped many women, including myself, overcome imposter syndrome and pursue their tech interests with confidence.

## Future Aspirations

Moving forward, I hope to:

- Contribute more actively to community projects
- Mentor newcomers to the tech field
- Potentially speak at future GDG events to share my knowledge
- Collaborate on innovative wearable technology solutions

## Conclusion

Being part of GDG WOW Pune has been transformative for both my professional growth and personal development. If you're considering joining this community, I wholeheartedly encourage you to take the leap. The supportive environment, learning opportunities, and connections you'll make are truly valuable in today's tech landscape.
